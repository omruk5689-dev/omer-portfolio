Hi, I'm Omer. I'm 18 years old and I'm obsessed with hardware. I design PCBs — from tiny dev boards and macropads to flight controllers and rocket avionics — then write the firmware that brings them to life.

I love the whole loop: schematic, layout, solder, debug, repeat. Most of my projects start with "this should be simple" and end with a new revision on the bench.

THINGS I LOVE

  • + PCB design & layout
  • + Custom hardware
  • + Embedded C firmware
  • + Hand soldering & rework
  • + 3D-printed enclosures
  • + Making LEDs blink
